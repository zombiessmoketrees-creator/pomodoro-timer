## Script

You can out-work almost anyone for a week. You cannot out-work **your own biology** for a year.

## The cost

After 17 hours awake, your reaction time drops to the level of someone who is legally impaired. Most people call that a *normal Tuesday*.

> [!stat] 17 hours
> awake is where measurable impairment begins

## What sleep actually does

- Moves the day's learning into **long-term memory**
- Clears metabolic waste from the brain
- Resets the emotional centres that drive bad decisions

> Sleep is the single most effective thing we can do to reset our brain and body health each day.
> — Matthew Walker

## The fix

Pick a fixed wake-up time and defend it for two weeks. Everything else — caffeine cut-offs, cool rooms, [[Morning Light|morning light]] — is an optimisation on top of that one habit.
